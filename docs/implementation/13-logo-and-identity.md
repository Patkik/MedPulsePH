# 13. Logo and identity brief

**Status:** Planning requirements; implementation not started.

[Plan index](../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) | [Iteration packages](ITERATIONS.md) | Primary package: I03

---

Working name: **MedPulsePH**. Use one recognizable geometric idea rather than a cross, heartbeat, shield, and NFC waves combined. Develop the mark in monochrome and test its physical and digital applications.

| Direction | Single idea | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clasp — recommended** | Geometric fastening joint of a wristband, with an intentional negative-space channel. | Must avoid resembling generic chain links. |
| Folded M | A continuous folded band forms an M. | Common monogram; proportions must be distinctive. |
| Open aperture | A controlled opening in a solid geometric boundary. | May look too architectural without a strong wordmark. |

For the clasp, start on a 64-by-64 integer grid with an eight-unit module and critical negative-space channels at least eight units wide. Use consistent geometry, remove unnecessary details, keep the wordmark one color with deliberate kerning, and keep `PH` legible without a separate accent badge. These are proposed construction constraints, not a finished mark.

Before acceptance, render and inspect black on white, white on black, actual 16- and 24-pixel sizes, a construction-grid overlay, and an eight-millimeter physical engraving or equivalent sample. Inspect large signage and perform a subtraction check. Produce vector mark, horizontal lockup, monochrome variants, PWA icon compositions, clear-space/minimum-size rules, and physical-use guidance after visual review. No logo artwork has been created yet.
